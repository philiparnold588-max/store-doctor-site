Store Doctor

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 September 2026

Store Doctor is operated by Excel Web. This Privacy Policy explains how Store Doctor accesses, uses, and protects information when merchants install and use the app.

1. Information Store Doctor Accesses

Store Doctor accesses only the Shopify store information necessary to provide its product template management functionality.

This may include:

  • Product titles and product status
  • Product template assignments
  • Theme template information
  • Store identification information required for authentication
  • App installation and session information

Store Doctor does not access or store protected customer data such as customer names, postal addresses, email addresses, phone numbers, payment information, or customer order details.

2. How Information Is Used

Store Doctor uses accessed information to:

  • Display products and their assigned templates
  • Search and filter products
  • Allow merchants to update product template assignments
  • Perform bulk product template changes
  • Authenticate the merchant's Shopify store
  • Maintain and operate the Store Doctor service

Store Doctor does not sell merchant or customer information.

3. Data Sharing

Store Doctor does not sell, rent, or trade merchant data.

Information may be shared with service providers where necessary to operate the app, such as hosting or infrastructure providers, and only to the extent required to provide the service.

Information may also be disclosed where required by law, regulation, court order, or other valid legal request.

4. Data Retention

Store Doctor retains only the information necessary to operate and maintain the app.

App installation and session information may be retained while the app is installed.

When Store Doctor is uninstalled, associated app data is handled in accordance with Shopify's applicable data deletion and privacy requirements.

5. Shopify Privacy Webhooks

Store Doctor supports Shopify's mandatory privacy and deletion webhook requirements, including applicable data request, customer redaction, and shop redaction requests.

6. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organisational safeguards are used to protect information processed by Store Doctor against unauthorised access, misuse, loss, alteration, or disclosure.

7. Shopify

Store Doctor operates within the Shopify platform and uses Shopify APIs. Merchants should also review Shopify's own privacy practices and terms.

8. Your Rights

Depending on applicable law, merchants may have rights relating to personal information, including rights of access, correction, deletion, restriction, or objection.

Store Doctor does not process protected Shopify customer data as part of its normal app functionality.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to Store Doctor, legal requirements, or Shopify requirements. The latest version will be published on this page.
